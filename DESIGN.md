# Design System

## Overview

Outtcast should feel like a high-end performance product with fashion-editorial restraint. The app is light-performance, proof-first, and private by design. It should feel more like a members-only athletic editorial than a wellness tracker, creator feed, or productivity dashboard.

The visual thesis is:

- high-end performance
- light-performance base
- soft luxury mineral contrast
- mineral blue palette
- fashion-editorial energy
- proof-first hierarchy
- editorial top, technical body

Every important screen should create desire first, then resolve into clarity. The top of the screen should feel atmospheric and branded. The body should feel sharper, calmer, and more instrument-like.

## Creative North Star

Design for a private members club for people who are serious about improving their lives. The app should feel selective, aspirational, and culturally aware, but never smug. Social energy should feel polished and supportive. Progress should feel earned, visible, and structured.

The product should not feel like:

- a soft pastel wellness app
- a generic SaaS dashboard
- a creator-social feed
- a gamified habit toy
- a chat product with accountability bolted on

## Color And Material

Use a cool mineral system with soft tonal layering instead of hard contrast or heavy outlines.

- `surface.base`: `#F8F9FF`
- `surface.section`: `#EFF4FF`
- `surface.card`: `#E5EEFF`
- `surface.active`: `#D3E4FE`
- `ink.primary`: `#0B1C30`
- `ink.secondary`: `#4B5B70`
- `accent.primary`: `#00288E`
- `accent.support`: `#446273`
- `accent.rare`: `#F3E0CA`

Rules:

- Use pale mineral gray and soft off-white as the default canvas.
- Use deep graphite for text instead of pure black.
- Use rich cobalt for the most important action or focus state.
- Use steel-teal for secondary structure, progress support, and quieter emphasis.
- Use champagne rarely, only for milestones, seals, premium confirmation, or elevated moments.
- Prefer tonal layering over borders. If a boundary is needed, make it faint.
- Avoid rainbow accents, loud gradients, or purple-heavy palettes.

## Typography

- `Display / editorial`: `Cormorant Garamond`
- `Body / labels / metrics / controls`: `Manrope`

Usage rules:

- Use `Cormorant Garamond` for hero headlines, mission lines, welcome statements, and occasional section-leading moments.
- Use `Manrope` for everything functional: body copy, labels, cards, controls, metrics, chips, tabs, and account surfaces.
- Treat the serif as a signature voice, not a dominant voice.
- Keep metric type precise, clean, and readable.
- Use stronger typographic contrast between the editorial top and the technical body.

## Shape And Layout

The shape language is `sharp shell, soft touch`.

- Hero panels and main layout shells should feel tailored and architectural.
- Buttons, pills, chips, and segmented controls can be slightly softer.
- Use a hero panel plus modular proof architecture on primary screens.
- Keep one dominant focal moment near the top of the screen.
- Use whitespace as structure. If a screen feels noisy, remove chrome before adding decoration.

Default posture:

- card radius: medium, tailored, premium
- controls: softer than cards, but still decisive
- no bubbly or cartoon-soft geometry

## Motion

Motion should feel like `precision glide` with a little `athletic snap` on taps and check-in actions.

- use calm transitions, fades, and measured slides for page and section changes
- use slightly tighter response for taps, segmented controls, and check-in confirmation
- keep primary motion around 200ms to 280ms
- make check-in publish feel like a soft ceremony, not a loud celebration
- avoid bouncy springs, playful overshoot, and novelty animation

## Imagery And Backgrounds

Use imagery mixed sparingly.

- Favor mineral wash backgrounds and abstract editorial texture.
- Use tightly cropped performance photography only occasionally.
- Images should support mood, not dominate the layout.
- Do not make any main screen image-led by default.

## Component Language

### Buttons

- Primary buttons should feel decisive, cobalt-led, and slightly satin.
- Secondary buttons should use tonal fills or quiet outlines.
- Tertiary actions should stay minimal and editorial.

### Cards

- Cards should feel like proof objects, not social posts.
- Use tailored evidence cards with metrics first, caption second, support signals third.
- Avoid divider-heavy card construction.

### Metrics

- Metrics should feel `sport luxury`.
- Prioritize strong numbers, quiet labels, slim progress tracks, and structured callouts.
- Avoid game HUD styling, rings everywhere, or over-decorated dashboards.

### Controls

- Controls should feel `confident performance`.
- Selected states must be obvious, but still premium.
- Segmented controls and chips should feel tactile and clean.

### Icons

- Use minimal line icons only.
- Keep them thin to medium stroke.
- Icons support navigation and state; they are not the screen personality.

### Navigation

- Use a tailored dock, not a default tab strip.
- `Check-in` should be a subtle centerpiece, not a loud floating action button.

## Screen Personalities

### Onboarding

- curated invitation
- curated interview
- belonging-led, with ambition underneath

### Home

- private members club
- led by your momentum
- tailored evidence cards

### Check-In

- fast ritual
- progressive reveal
- soft ceremony at publish

### Profile

- momentum board
- mission-led hero
- structured proof board

### Squads

- inner circle studio
- selected squad identity
- studio roster plus room preview

### Account And Provider Surfaces

- private control surface
- calm, trustworthy, and explicit
- honest about connected, disconnected, stale, and limited states

## Product Rules

- Proof comes before vanity.
- Metrics lead. Captions support.
- Social cues should feel warm and restrained, not noisy.
- Editorial energy belongs at the top of the screen; the body should stay clearer and more technical.
- Health, privacy, and visibility context must stay legible and trustworthy.

## Do

- Do make every primary screen feel premium within three seconds.
- Do use Cormorant for emotional identity and Manrope for utility.
- Do keep the palette disciplined and cool.
- Do use tonal layering, mineral wash, and whitespace to create depth.
- Do keep proof cards structured and easy to scan.
- Do make onboarding feel desirable instead of form-like.

## Don't

- Don't make the app look like Instagram, TikTok, Discord, or a wellness template.
- Don't use oversized avatars, loud badge systems, or public-social vanity counters.
- Don't rely on hard divider lines or flat utility cards everywhere.
- Don't make Check-in feel like a generic composer or form wizard.
- Don't let Profile drift into a bio page or self-branding surface without proof.
