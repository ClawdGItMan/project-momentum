# Soul

## Mission

Make self-improvement feel social, visible, and culturally magnetic.

## Cultural Thesis

Current social platforms are optimized for escape, comparison, and passive consumption. Project Momentum exists to flip that dynamic. The product should make it feel cool to get better, cool to show up consistently, and cool to support people who are genuinely trying to improve their lives.

## Target Users

- people already invested in health, fitness, or personal growth who want accountability
- users of apps like Strava, Whoop, or habit trackers who currently manage progress across disconnected tools
- small creators sharing authentic improvement journeys who want fairer distribution than algorithmic vanity platforms

## Transformation Promise

Project Momentum helps people move from private intention to public momentum. Users should feel:

- more accountable
- more encouraged
- more consistent
- more connected to people on a similar path
- less trapped by performative, low-value social media

## Tone

The brand should feel:

- disciplined but not preachy
- ambitious but not cringe
- positive without becoming fake
- direct, contemporary, and earned through action

## Non-Negotiables

- progress is rewarded relative to the user’s own baseline, not just against elites
- health and fitness are the first wedge, but the long-term vision is broader self-improvement
- social accountability matters more than isolated tracking
- consistency matters more than flashy one-off wins
- the product should encourage action, not doomscrolling

## Anti-Goals

Project Momentum should not become:

- another vanity content feed
- a finance flex leaderboard
- a shame machine for low performers
- a generic all-in-one life dashboard with no emotional pull
- a manipulative streak app that confuses compulsion for progress

## Why This Should Exist Now

- health, fitness, and self-improvement are culturally strong and highly shareable
- authentic small creators struggle to reach people on current platforms
- users already generate improvement data across many apps, but lack a social accountability layer
- strong app-store ratings for imperfect products suggest the demand exists before the category leader does

## What Success Feels Like

Someone opens Project Momentum and immediately thinks: these are my kind of people, this makes me want to do the work, and posting progress here feels better than posting for shallow validation anywhere else.
