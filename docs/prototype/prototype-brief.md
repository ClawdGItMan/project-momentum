# Prototype Brief

## Purpose

Build a strong iPhone-first prototype that makes the value of Project Momentum obvious within one session. The prototype should feel like a real social product, not a wireframe exercise.

## What The Prototype Must Prove

- users understand the idea quickly
- the selective social accountability angle feels differentiated
- progress posting feels more meaningful than posting on generic social apps
- health-first self-improvement feels like a strong enough wedge
- the UI feels premium and memorable enough that users want to spend time in it
- Apple Health connectivity makes the product feel grounded in real behavior rather than pure manual logging

## Primary Audience For The Prototype

- existing self-improvement users
- fitness and wellness app users
- early creator-style users who share progress content already

## Prototype Constraints

- optimize for speed and clarity over backend completeness
- use believable mock data where integrations are not yet essential, but keep Apple Health on the critical path
- use at most one real integration unless a second one is clearly worth the sprint cost
- design for App Store demo quality, but do not overbuild gamification systems

## Prototype Pillars

### Clear identity

Users should immediately see that this is for people trying to improve their lives.

### Daily action loop

The core loop should be easy to understand: track, post, encourage, repeat.

### Visible momentum

Profiles and feeds should show progress signals, not just text posts.

### Social proof

Users should be able to add friends and feel the app is better with peers inside it.

### Premium feel

The product should look intentional, interactive, and polished with a premium-fitness feel: restrained color, strong hierarchy, clean surfaces, and a few meaningful animations.

## Deliverables

- onboarding experience
- profile setup and profile view
- habit and metrics surfaces
- consistency signal and status surfaces
- daily progress post flow
- feed and social interaction flow
- visibility controls and friend management basics
- squad-first accountability surfaces

## Demo Story

The strongest demo path is:

1. onboard with self-improvement goals
2. set up a profile and choose focus areas
3. connect Apple Health, with manual entry only as fallback
4. choose whether a post is for friends or a specific squad
5. post a daily progress update
6. view a feed of peers making visible progress
7. leave feeling that this is where improvement-minded people belong
