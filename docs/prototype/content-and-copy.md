# Content And Copy

## Voice Principles

- confident, not corny
- motivating, not preachy
- modern, not pseudo-spiritual
- earned through action, not hype

## Messaging Direction

The app should sound like a place where disciplined people support each other, not like a motivational poster generator.

## Suggested Core Language

- momentum
- progress
- accountability
- consistency
- focus
- build
- show the work

## Words To Use Carefully

- grind
- hustle
- alpha
- discipline
- transformation

These words may be useful in specific contexts, but they can become cringe or exclusionary if overused.

## Onboarding Copy Starters

### Welcome

"Build a life worth showing up for."

### Thesis

"Project Momentum is where progress gets social."

### Goal selection prompt

"What are you actively trying to improve right now?"

### Accountability prompt

"Who do you become when your effort is visible?"

### Post prompt

"Show the work."

## Profile Content Ideas

- short mission line
- current focus pillars
- weekly momentum summary
- recent wins

## Feed Content Rules

- progress posts should be structured enough to skim
- captions should add context, not replace the progress signal
- reactions should feel like encouragement, not vanity scoring
