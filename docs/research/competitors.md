# Competitor Research

## Direct And Adjacent Competitors

### Lock In

- strongest direct signal from current notes
- combines self-improvement framing with gamified progression
- appears to include character UI, leveling, and ranking concepts
- key weakness from current understanding: lacks true social accountability and leans more toward community chat than friend-based progress sharing
- important signal: strong App Store ratings suggest real appetite even with weak UI and UX

### Strava

- best-known social fitness benchmark
- strong social proof around workouts and consistency
- weakness for Project Momentum comparison: limited mainly to fitness activities and not designed as a broader identity layer for self-improvement

### Whoop

- valuable for recovery, sleep, and readiness metrics
- trusted as a data source, not a social destination
- can act as an integration partner more than a head-on competitor

### Instagram And TikTok

- not direct category competitors in feature shape, but major behavioral competitors
- users already post progress there, yet the environment rewards aesthetics, virality, and distraction more than real improvement
- the emotional comparison effect is high and accountability is weak

## What To Learn Next

- what Lock In users praise most and complain about most
- whether users want broad self-improvement now or prefer a health-first wedge
- what types of social interactions feel motivating instead of cringe
- which adjacent products users would actually connect to a new app

## Signal

There appears to be open space between narrow tracking apps and general social media: users want motivation and visibility around growth, but current products either silo the data or flatten it into generic content.

## Implication

Project Momentum should win first on socially meaningful progress sharing, not by trying to out-feature every tracker or out-entertain every social platform.

## Next Step

Deep-dive Lock In reviews, document side-by-side feature comparisons, and validate which social features users actually wish these products had.
