# Interview Plan

## Objective

Validate whether the target users actually want a dedicated social self-improvement app, what they would share there, and which mechanics would make it feel meaningfully better than existing options.

## Priority Segments

### Segment 1: fitness and wellness users

- people already using Strava, Whoop, or habit trackers
- friends with existing routines and measurable goals

### Segment 2: authentic small creators

- creators with roughly 500 to 1000 followers
- people posting real fitness, wellness, or growth content without strong algorithmic reach

### Segment 3: socially motivated improvers

- users who want accountability from friends
- people who find solo habit tracking hard to sustain

## Named Starting Targets

- Mary
- co-founder sibling or brother who is already into self-improvement
- fitness-focused friends already using tracking products
- a small set of creators reached through TikTok outreach

## Core Questions

1. What do you currently track about your health or self-improvement?
2. Where do you share progress today, if anywhere?
3. What feels missing from Strava, Whoop, habit trackers, Instagram, or TikTok?
4. Would you want friends to see your habits or metrics? Which ones?
5. What would make posting progress feel motivating instead of embarrassing?
6. What would make you come back daily?
7. Which integrations would actually matter to you?
8. What parts of gamification feel motivating, and what parts feel childish or manipulative?

## Interview Notes Format

For every interview entry in `interview-log.md`, capture:

- participant type
- current tools
- behaviors today
- desired outcomes
- objections
- memorable quotes
- `Signal`
- `Implication`
- `Next Step`

## Recruiting Goal

- 5 to 8 interviews in the first week
- at least 2 creator conversations
- at least 3 users already using fitness or recovery trackers

## Signal

The fastest way to de-risk this product is not broad survey data; it is direct conversations with people already living the behavior this app wants to amplify.

## Implication

Interview quality matters more than interview volume. The first round should focus on users who already care about visible progress and accountability.

## Next Step

Schedule the first interviews immediately and log them in a consistent format so themes can be compared quickly.
