# Personas

## Persona 1: The Accountable Athlete

### Snapshot

- already works out consistently
- uses one or more fitness or recovery apps
- wants visible accountability and social reinforcement

### Pain Points

- progress lives in too many places
- current social apps feel performative or distracting
- existing fitness apps do not capture the full self-improvement identity

### What They Need

- a better place to post progress
- a profile that shows momentum, not just photos
- friends who reinforce consistency

## Persona 2: The Authentic Small Creator

### Snapshot

- posts fitness, wellness, or growth content
- has a modest following and weak algorithmic reach
- wants recognition for real effort rather than platform games

### Pain Points

- quality effort does not guarantee distribution
- current platforms reward trend chasing over substance
- community engagement is shallow and inconsistent

### What They Need

- a platform where consistent progress earns visibility
- a feed with more aligned audiences
- a way to grow alongside the people they inspire

## Persona 3: The Growth-Minded Friend Group Member

### Snapshot

- wants better habits
- is motivated by peers more than solo tracking
- may be less hardcore than the athlete persona but still wants progress

### Pain Points

- habit trackers feel lonely or clinical
- mainstream social media makes them consume more than act
- goal setting fades without visible accountability

### What They Need

- simple daily posting and habit check-ins
- encouragement from people they actually know
- a place where trying is normal and socially rewarded

## Early Adopter Priority

Start with people who already care about self-improvement. Do not spend the first sprint trying to convert users who are currently uninterested in health, growth, or accountability.
