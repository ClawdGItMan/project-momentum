# Product Vision

## Product Idea

Project Momentum is a social self-improvement app where people share progress, stay accountable, and build identity around getting better. It combines progress tracking, simple social mechanics, and selective integrations into one place that feels more constructive than mainstream social media and more motivating than a private tracker.

## Core Promise

If a user is doing the work, the app should help that effort feel visible, supported, and worth sharing.

## North-Star Outcome

Users come back because the app helps them act on their goals, not because it traps their attention. The strongest sign of success is when someone does a healthy behavior partly because they want to post the progress to a community that values real effort.

## Experience Principles

- Social first, but grounded in real progress
- Improvement across life, starting with health and fitness
- Baseline-relative rewards to keep the experience motivating
- Public encouragement over empty validation
- Friction low enough for daily use, but meaningful enough to avoid becoming fluff

## Product Shape Over Time

### Near term

- personal progress feed
- profile-based accountability
- habits and metrics in one place
- shareable daily updates

### Mid term

- stronger gamification and character progression
- better discovery of like-minded people
- smarter ranking and visibility systems

### Longer term

- local groups and map-based coordination
- deeper integration ecosystem
- broader life-category coverage beyond health

## Why People Might Switch

- existing tracking apps are siloed
- mainstream social apps reward aesthetics and virality more than effort
- users want social proof and encouragement around healthy routines
- small creators want a fairer platform where consistent work actually matters
