# Open Questions

## Product

- What should the final app name be?
- How should consistency be visualized and explained in the first experience?
- How prominent should milestones and levels be relative to consistency?

## Research

- What are Lock In users actually praising and complaining about in public reviews?
- Which integrations do target users care about enough to influence adoption beyond Apple Health?
- What makes progress posting feel motivating rather than embarrassing?
- What creator pain points are severe enough to drive switching behavior?

## Prototype

- Should Strava be adapter-backed only or become the second live provider in v0.1?
- How should consistency be calculated and rendered on profile, feed, and habits screens?
- What is the minimal set of encouragement interactions needed for the feed?
- Which screens need to feel “App Store ready” versus merely functional?

## Go-To-Market

- Is the first wedge friend groups, accountability circles, or creator-led communities?
- What is the most believable story for why users invite others?
- Which content examples will make the concept click fastest during early testing?

## Decision Pressure

These questions should be answered in the order that most reduces prototype risk. Not every question needs a final answer before building, but the ones that affect the first-run experience should be resolved early.
